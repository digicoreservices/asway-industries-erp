1) Project Title
Asway Industries ERP - An Enterprise Resource Planning system designed for industrial manufacturing and distribution.

3) Type of Project
Web-based Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) System for manufacturing and distribution businesses. It's a full-stack React application with cloud-based backend infrastructure.

4) Scope Description
Asway Industries ERP streamlines complete business operations from customer engagement through production and financial management. It handles:
	Customer and supplier relationship management
	Inventory and procurement tracking
	Sales quotations and purchase orders
	Production planning and work order management
	Manufacturing cost calculations
	GST-compliant invoicing and payment tracking
	Financial analytics and reporting
The system is designed to eliminate manual processes and provide real-time visibility across all business operations.

5) Modules Specification
A. Relationship Management
	Client Management (customer data, departments, contacts, GST details)
	Supplier Management (vendor information and performance tracking)

B. Inventory & Procurement
	Item Management (raw materials, finished goods, GST calculations)
	Purchase Orders from Suppliers (procurement tracking with taxes and charges)
C. Sales & Production
	Quotation Management (sales and service quotes)
	Purchase Order Receipts (customer orders with target planning)
	Work Orders (production tasks with time and labor tracking)
	Manufacturing (raw material utilization and cost calculations)

D. Finance & Compliance
	Invoicing (GST-compliant with HSN codes)
	Payment Receipts (customer payments with tax deductions)
	Supplier Payments (vendor payment management)
E. Analytics & Reporting
	Customer-wise analysis
	Product-wise analysis
	Delivery period tracking
	Value and user performance metrics

6) Tools and Technologies
Frontend:
	React 18.2.0 (UI framework)
	Vite (build tool)
	TailwindCSS 3.3.2 (styling)
	shadcn/ui & Radix UI (component library)
	React Router 6.16.0 (navigation)
	Framer Motion (animations)
	Lucide React (icons)
	React Hook Form (form handling)
	jsPDF & html2canvas (PDF generation)
	date-fns (date management)

7) Backend & Database:
	Supabase (Backend-as-a-Service)
	PostgreSQL (database)
	Supabase Auth (authentication)
	Supabase Storage (file storage)
	Edge Functions/Deno (server-side logic)

8) Security:
	Row Level Security (RLS) policies
	Protected routes and authentication
	Client-side validation
